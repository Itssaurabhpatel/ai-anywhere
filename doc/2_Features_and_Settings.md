# AI Keyboard: Features & Settings Guide

This document covers all the features available in the AI Keyboard and explains how to customize your typing experience through settings.

---

## 1. Core Keyboard Features

### 🧠 Ask AI & Inline Generation
- **Toolbar Toggle**: Tap the AI avatar icon on the keyboard toolbar to open the AI input panel.
- **Ask Anything**: Type a prompt (e.g., "Write a polite email declining an offer") and the AI will generate the text right inside the keyboard. You can then insert it directly into your chat or email app.

### ✍️ AI Summarize & Reply
- Select any text in your current app. The keyboard can analyze the selected text and automatically suggest smart, context-aware replies or summarize long paragraphs for you.

### 🌐 Quick Translate
- Tap the translate icon in the toolbar. Type in your native language, and the AI will instantly translate it into your chosen target language before inserting it into the app.

### 📖 N-Gram Word Suggestions
- The keyboard features an advanced, dynamic N-Gram suggestion engine. It learns the pairs of words you type (e.g., "how" -> "are") and predicts your next words in the center pill of the suggestion bar, speeding up your typing dramatically.

### 🎤 Voice Typing (Speech-to-Text)
- Tap the microphone icon on the toolbar to dictate text hands-free.

### 🖼️ GIF & Media Search
- Search and insert animated GIFs directly from the keyboard toolbar to make your conversations more expressive.

---

## 2. Keyboard Settings & Customization

You can access these options by tapping the **Settings (Gear) Icon** on the keyboard toolbar.

### 🎨 Theme & Appearance
- **Backgrounds**: Choose from solid colors, gradients, or set a custom photo from your gallery as the keyboard background.
- **Key Sizes**: Adjust the size of the keys to Small, Normal, Large, or Extra Large depending on your finger size and screen preference.
- **Key Borders**: Toggle key outlines on or off for a flat or raised look.
- **Number Row**: Choose whether to constantly display a dedicated number row (1-0) above the standard letters.

### ⚙️ Typing Preferences
- **Auto-correction**: Automatically fixes minor typos as you press the spacebar.
- **Swipe/Finger Typing**: Allows you to glide your finger across the letters to form words without lifting it.
- **Auto-capitalization**: Automatically capitalizes the first letter of a new sentence.
- **Word Suggestions**: Toggles the top predictive text bar on or off.

### 📳 Feedback Settings
- **Vibrate on keypress (Haptics)**: Provides physical feedback when you tap a key.
- **Sound on keypress**: Plays a subtle click sound while typing.
- **Popup on keypress**: Shows an enlarged preview of the letter you are currently pressing to ensure accuracy.

### 🤖 AI Preferences
- **Tone & Style**: Set default instructions for how the AI should reply (e.g., "Always reply in a professional tone" or "Make it sound casual").
- **Language**: Set the default output language for your AI tasks.
